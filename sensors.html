1 · How the system works

Sense → Decide → Act

Sensors turn physical quantities (pressure, temperature, position, speed) into electrical signals. The ECU compares them with stored maps and drives actuators such as injectors, coils and motors.

Signal types

Analog voltage (0.5–4.5 V), digital / Hall square waves, AC / VR sine waves, PWM / frequency, and CAN / LIN data messages.

The 3-wire rule

Most active sensors share a regulated 5 V reference, a signal wire and a clean sensor ground that returns to the ECU, not to the chassis. Two-wire thermistors use only signal and sensor ground.

Diagnosing

Check reference voltage (5 V ±0.1), ground drop (<50 mV), then sweep the signal while moving the input. Use a scope on CKP, CMP and knock; keep shielded pairs grounded at the ECU end only.
